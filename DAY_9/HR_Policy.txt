COMPANY HR POLICY DOCUMENT

========================================
1. ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY

All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Employees who join the company during the year will receive annual leave on a prorated basis.

Annual leave requests should be submitted at least 3 working days before the planned leave date.

Employees must obtain approval from their reporting manager before taking annual leave.

Unused annual leave can be carried forward to the next calendar year, subject to company policy.

========================================
2. SICK LEAVE POLICY

Employees are entitled to 12 days of sick leave per calendar year.

If an employee is absent due to illness for more than 2 consecutive working days, a medical certificate may be required.

Employees should inform their reporting manager as soon as possible when they are unable to attend work due to illness.

Sick leave cannot be converted into annual leave.

========================================
3. MATERNITY LEAVE POLICY

Eligible employees are entitled to 26 weeks of maternity leave.

Employees should notify the HR department about planned maternity leave as early as possible.

Maternity leave requests must be submitted through the company's HR portal.

Additional leave may be available depending on applicable company policies and regulations.

========================================
4. PATERNITY LEAVE POLICY

Eligible employees can take 15 days of paternity leave.

Paternity leave should generally be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.

Employees should submit their paternity leave request to their reporting manager and HR department.

========================================
5. WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Employees may request work from home depending on their role and business requirements.

Work from home requests must be approved by the reporting manager.

Employees working remotely are expected to remain available during normal working hours.

Employees must attend important meetings scheduled during their working hours.

========================================
6. WORKING HOURS

The standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM from Monday to Friday.

Employees are expected to complete their assigned responsibilities during working hours.

Flexible working hours may be available for eligible employees with manager approval.

========================================
7. NOTICE PERIOD

Employees who resign from the company must serve a standard notice period of 60 days.

The notice period begins from the date the resignation is formally accepted.

Employees should complete knowledge transfer and return company assets before their final working day.

========================================
8. EMPLOYEE BENEFITS


Full-time employees are eligible for health insurance benefits according to the company's benefits policy.

Employees may also be eligible for retirement benefits depending on their employment terms.

The HR department can provide detailed information about available employee benefits.

========================================
9. PERFORMANCE REVIEW

Employee performance reviews are conducted twice a year.

Performance evaluations consider individual goals, team contributions, technical skills, and overall performance.

Employees should discuss their performance goals with their reporting managers.

========================================
10. TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT

Employees may participate in company-sponsored training programs.

Employees should discuss relevant training opportunities with their reporting manager.

The company may provide access to technical courses, leadership programs, and professional development resources.

========================================
11. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT

Employees can request reimbursement for eligible business expenses.

Expense claims should be submitted within 30 days of incurring the expense.

Employees must provide valid receipts and supporting documents for reimbursement.

All expense claims are subject to manager approval and company reimbursement policies.

========================================
12. COMPANY ASSETS


Employees are responsible for company-provided laptops, mobile devices, access cards, and other equipment.

Company assets must be returned when an employee leaves the organization.

Employees should immediately report lost or damaged company property to the IT or HR department.
